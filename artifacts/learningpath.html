Artifact Detail

Role Based Learning Path

A structured learning model that organized ScoopRoute training around the responsibilities, permissions, and workflows of five distinct customer personas.

Artifact Brief

Key context for this sample

This artifact demonstrates how I helped transform product area training into a more practical role based learning experience. The existing training content was organized by product area rather than by persona or workflow. That structure made sense internally, but it required learners to determine which courses applied to their responsibilities and in what order they should complete them. I worked with two learning experience designers to validate the personas, map each role to the workflows they performed, review the course structure, recommend lesson sequencing, identify content gaps, and revise learning content. The resulting paths connected shared foundational content with the tasks and responsibilities most relevant to each persona.

Artifact Story

Situation

ScoopRoute customers used the same platform in very different ways depending on their roles. Business owners, administrators, route planners, customer service representatives, and delivery staff each needed a different combination of product knowledge.

The existing training was organized by product area. Learners could find courses about mobile tools, account management, routing, billing, or reporting, but they still had to decide which content applied to their job and what order made sense.

This approach created an onboarding experience that treated different users too similarly. It also made it harder to scale customer onboarding, support continued product adoption, and help learners move beyond isolated features toward complete job workflows.

Artifact Story

Task

I needed to help two learning experience designers create learning paths that reflected how customers actually used ScoopRoute.

My role included validating the personas and their workflows, reviewing the course structure, recommending lesson sequences, identifying documentation and training gaps, and editing learning content.

The paths needed to show each learner what to complete, what knowledge was required for their role, and how individual product features connected to their daily responsibilities. They also needed to reduce dependence on instructor led onboarding while still supporting new users and helping existing customers adopt more of the platform.

Artifact Story

Action

I reviewed help center documentation, existing courses, and subject matter expert guidance to understand the workflows associated with each persona.

We identified five primary personas and examined their daily responsibilities, product permissions, support needs, and onboarding expectations. Instead of assigning only unique tasks to each role, we mapped a combination of core workflows and role specific responsibilities.

Shared courses were linked across multiple learning paths rather than recreated for each persona. Each path was then organized around role responsibilities, beginning with basic navigation and moving into advanced workflows, less frequent tasks, and administrative responsibilities.

I worked closely with the learning experience designers through planning sessions, content mapping workshops, shared documents, comments, direct editing, asynchronous reviews, and informal discussions. They led the learning architecture, objectives, assessments, video recommendations, and interactive design. I contributed product and documentation context, validated the workflow mapping, and helped make sure the course sequence matched the way customers used the platform.

The learning paths included knowledge checks, hands on exercises, product simulations, and final assessments. Existing documentation supplied approved source content, deeper reference material, shared terminology, and coverage for topics that did not require a full lesson.

The review process also uncovered several documentation gaps and areas where existing lessons needed revision. We added missing content, changed lesson sequences, and clarified role descriptions where the original structure did not accurately reflect the learner journey.

Artifact Story

Result

The completed paths gave customers a clearer way to navigate ScoopRoute training according to their responsibilities.

Learners received more focused onboarding, clearer academy navigation, and a stronger connection between training and the tasks they were expected to perform. The project also created a prioritized roadmap for missing content, improved reuse of existing courses, and revealed where documentation or training needed additional coverage.

Over time, the model supported more scalable onboarding and more consistent role based training. It also created a reusable framework that could be adapted for future personas without rebuilding the learning system from the beginning.

The project demonstrated how persona analysis, workflow mapping, information architecture, documentation strategy, and instructional design can work together to make training more practical and easier to maintain.
