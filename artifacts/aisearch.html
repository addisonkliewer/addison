AI Search Optimization

An analysis and content improvement project that used AI search data to identify documentation gaps, strengthen article structure, and improve the quality of answers returned to users.

Artifact Brief

Key context for this sample

I treated AI search performance as a content quality problem, not just a search problem. Working with Rhea, I used Doc360 analytics to identify weak answers across troubleshooting, procedural, and release related searches, then traced those issues to gaps in the docs, including missing content, inconsistent terminology, unclear titles, weak information architecture, and poorly structured articles. I used those findings to improve individual SR articles and strengthen the broader content system.

Artifact Story

Situation

I worked on a product whose AI search experience depended on a large documentation set covering procedural guidance, troubleshooting information, release updates, and product concepts.

I found that the search tool could retrieve and summarize existing information, but it couldn't compensate for missing articles, inconsistent terminology, vague titles, or content without a clear structure. When users searched for troubleshooting help or asked how to complete a workflow, an unsuccessful answer often pointed to a deeper documentation problem rather than a limitation of the AI alone.

I needed a more systematic way to understand why searches failed and which content improvements would have the greatest effect.

Artifact Story

Task

I needed to evaluate AI search performance and translate the findings into practical documentation improvements.

I went beyond identifying unsuccessful searches. I determined whether each issue came from a missing article, inconsistent product language, weak information architecture, insufficient metadata, an unclear title, or content that didn't state the answer directly enough for reliable retrieval.

I aimed to improve the search experience while also strengthening the documentation for human readers, future maintenance, and reuse across other AI supported workflows.

Artifact Story

Action

I collaborated with Rhea to review Doc360 search analytics and evaluate the questions users asked. We focused on troubleshooting, procedural how-to, and release-related queries because these searches often reflected an immediate user need and exposed clear gaps in the content system. I looked at Scoop Route, she looked at our sister product.

I grouped unsuccessful and low-quality responses by likely cause. Some searches revealed that the necessary documentation didn't exist. Others showed that the information existed but was hard to retrieve because articles used inconsistent terminology, unclear titles, weak taxonomy, limited metadata, or structures that buried the answer.

I used these findings to prioritize new documentation and recommend revisions to existing content. I created missing articles, aligned terminology with the language users searched for, strengthened titles and headings, made procedures easier to scan, added clearer context, and organized related information more consistently.

Rather than optimizing content only for an AI tool, I focused on changes that improved both machine retrieval and the human reading experience.

Artifact Story

Result

I contributed to an approximately 50 percent improvement in successful AI search responses, measured through the search success rate in Doc360.

I saw the strongest gains from creating net-new documentation that had previously been missing and improving the structure of existing articles. Better titles, terminology, metadata, taxonomy, and information architecture gave the AI more reliable source material while also making the knowledge base easier for customers and internal teams to navigate directly.

I took away an important lesson: our AI search quality is built on documentation quality. When content is accurate, clearly named, consistently structured, and connected through useful metadata, it becomes stronger infrastructure for search, support, product education, and future AI assisted content workflows.
