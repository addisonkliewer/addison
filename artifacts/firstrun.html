In App Guidance Flow Sample: First Run

I created a first run Pendo guide that helped existing ScoopRoute customers understand and use a changed operations workflow when they encountered it in the product.

Artifact Brief

Key context for this sample

This artifact shows how I designed and built contextual guidance for an updated ScoopRoute workflow. The guide supported existing customers who already understood the feature but needed help adjusting to its changed behavior. Pendo entered our product workflow late in the development cycle, so I worked with a nearly finished interface and limited room for design changes. I wrote the microcopy, sequenced the steps, defined the audience and triggers, built the guide in Pendo, and tested it with Product before launch.

Artifact Story

Situation

ScoopRoute was preparing to release a change to an existing operations workflow. Customers understood the broader feature, but the new behavior changed how they entered or completed part of the process. Without guidance, they could rely on old habits, miss a new control, or become unsure about what would happen next.

Our team had only recently started using Pendo guides for release support. Because guidance was usually considered after development was mostly complete, I had to work around the existing interface rather than help shape it earlier.

Artifact Story

Task

I needed to create a short first run guide that introduced the change without slowing down experienced users. It had to appear only for affected customers, show them what was different, point to the most important controls, and explain the result of their actions.

I also needed to keep the experience focused. The guide could not repeat everything already visible in the interface or turn a small workflow change into a lengthy tutorial.

Artifact Story

Action

I worked with Juno, the Operations product manager, to confirm the target audience, intended behavior, launch timing, required actions, and known limitations. I relied primarily on product requirements and the nearly finished interface, then built the guide directly in Pendo as an introductory modal followed by three to five tooltips.

I wrote each step to stay short, clarify the next action, explain consequences where necessary, and match the terminology used in the interface and documentation. I defined the targeting and first run behavior, then previewed the guide in Pendo and tested it in staging with Product. Testing revealed an element targeting problem, which I corrected during two rounds of review.

Artifact Story

Result

The feature launched with contextual guidance in place. Existing customers received a clear introduction to the changed workflow without needing to leave the product or search for instructions.

We did not collect a formal adoption or support metric for this individual guide, but the completed experience was targeted, reviewed, tested, and ready at launch. The project also gave me practical experience with in app microcopy, first run sequencing, Pendo authoring, and content design within late stage product constraints.
