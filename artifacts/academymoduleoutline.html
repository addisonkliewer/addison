Artifact Story

Situation

At ScoopRoute, we needed to help new customers learn the full delivery workflow so their drivers could start delivering ice cream successfully. The information already existed across help articles, SME knowledge, and previous training recordings, but it lacked a clear learning sequence. As a result, drivers often trained one another on how to use the software.

We also needed broader academy content that could reduce recurring support questions, guide learners through complete workflows, and support different roles and experience levels without repeating the same information across multiple lessons.

Artifact Story

Task

I needed to help shape the source material into a scalable learning path that showed new customers what to do, why each step mattered, and where each task fit into the larger workflow.

The outline needed to define measurable learning objectives, organize lessons by role and real world task sequence, identify opportunities for practice and assessment, and connect each lesson to supporting documentation. It also needed to give instructional designers, writers, and subject matter experts enough structure to develop a learning experience that could extend beyond a single module.

Artifact Story

Action

I worked with instructional designers, subject matter experts, customer success teams, and other technical writers to review the modules and training materials. I helped coordinate the review process and helped apply feedback to identify missing scenarios, conflicting information, and areas where the content was too detailed for a new learner.

I organized the material by user role, then sequenced it around the stages of a real delivery workflow. Common tasks appeared before less frequent scenarios, while supporting concepts were introduced when learners needed them rather than presented as separate product explanations.

The outline included task and outcome based learning objectives, lesson descriptions, practice activities, knowledge checks, recommended demonstrations, supporting article links, and estimated completion times.

During review, I helped reorganize the sequence, simplify technical details, add missing scenarios, and divide longer lessons into more focused learning units.

Artifact Story

Result

I worked on the academy module outline to give writers, trainers, and subject matter experts a shared plan before course production began. I showed how each lesson connected to the full customer workflow, reduced duplicate work, and kept the training aligned with existing documentation.

I organized the course by user role so new customers could follow it more easily. I also linked related help articles so learners could find more detailed information after training and designed the structure for reuse in future courses.
