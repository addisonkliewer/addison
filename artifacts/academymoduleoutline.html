Artifact Story

Situation

New customers needed to learn a complete mobile driver workflow, but the supporting information existed across help articles, expert knowledge, and previous training recordings. The individual sources were accurate, but they had been created for different purposes and did not provide a clear learning sequence on their own.

Support teams continued to receive recurring questions, and the academy needed broader coverage that could guide learners through the entire workflow rather than explaining isolated features. The material also needed to support different roles and experience levels without duplicating the same information across multiple lessons.

Artifact Story

Task

I needed to help shape the source material into a scalable learning path that showed new customers what to do, why each step mattered, and where each task fit into the larger workflow.

The outline needed to define measurable learning objectives, organize lessons by role and real world task sequence, identify opportunities for practice and assessment, and connect each lesson to supporting documentation. It also needed to give instructional designers, writers, and subject matter experts enough structure to develop a learning experience that could extend beyond a single module.

Artifact Story

Action

I collaborated with instructional designers, subject matter experts, customer success teams, and other technical writers to review the existing documentation and training materials. I helped coordinate the review process and used feedback to identify missing scenarios, conflicting information, and areas where the content was too detailed for a new learner.

I organized the material by user role, then sequenced it around the stages of a real delivery workflow. Common tasks appeared before less frequent scenarios, while supporting concepts were introduced when learners needed them rather than presented as separate product explanations.

The outline included task and outcome based learning objectives, lesson descriptions, practice activities, knowledge checks, recommended demonstrations, supporting article links, and estimated completion times. During review, I helped reorganize the sequence, simplify technical details, add missing scenarios, and divide longer lessons into more focused learning units.

Artifact Story

Result

The outline gave the academy team a clearer learning progression and a shared development plan before production began. Writers, trainers, and subject matter experts could see how each lesson connected to the larger workflow, which reduced duplicated effort and helped keep the training consistent with existing documentation.

The role based structure made the material easier for new customers to follow, while the supporting article links gave learners a path to deeper information after completing the course. The approach also created a more reusable model for future academy content, allowing teams to transform accurate product knowledge into scalable training without rebuilding the structure for every new course.
