Docs to Training Transformation

A transformation of reference focused help content into a role based learning experience that guides new customers through customer account management workflows.

Artifact Brief

Key context for this sample

This artifact demonstrates how I helped turn existing help center documentation into structured training for new customers. The source material was accurate, but it had been written for users who needed to look up individual answers. It was spread across multiple articles, included advanced details that new learners did not yet need, and lacked the sequence, context, and practice required for training. I worked as a supporting technical writer, reviewer, editor, and project team member. I helped reshape the documentation into a self paced academy experience with video and walkthrough elements, while preserving the terminology and approved guidance used in the help center.

Artifact Story

Situation

New customers needed structured training on customer account management, but most of the available source material existed as help center articles. Those articles were useful when someone already knew what they needed to find, but they did not provide a clear path for learning the workflow from beginning to end.

The content was spread across several pages, assumed some existing product knowledge, included details better suited for later reference, and did not contain practice, assessment, or role specific guidance. The organization needed a way to scale onboarding, close a gap in the academy, and reduce the repeated questions reaching customer facing teams.

Artifact Story

Task

I needed to support the transformation of accurate reference documentation into a more focused learning experience for new customers.

The training needed to prioritize common tasks, align with learner goals, and retain the information that subject matter experts and trainers considered essential. It also needed to introduce concepts in a logical order, separate role specific guidance where necessary, and give learners ways to check their understanding rather than simply read through product instructions.

Artifact Story

Action

I reviewed the published help articles and worked with instructional designers, customer success teams, support teams, and academy leadership to identify which information belonged in the course.

We prioritized common customer tasks, learner goals, and expert recommendations. I helped remove repeated explanations, rare edge cases, and advanced workflows that learners could return to later in the help center.

The transformed content added learning objectives, knowledge checks, role specific context, and demonstration guidance that had not existed in the original articles. We first organized the material by user role, then arranged the lessons around the sequence in which learners would complete the work.

As a supporting technical writer and editor, I reviewed the training for accuracy, clarity, terminology, and alignment with the existing documentation. I also helped revise the sequence, shorten lessons, reduce unnecessary technical detail, separate content for different roles, and improve assessments during review.

Artifact Story

Result

The transformation created a more consistent and approachable onboarding experience while preserving the accuracy of the original documentation.

Learners received a guided progression instead of a collection of disconnected reference articles. The training supported better comprehension, stronger onboarding, and fewer repeated questions, while the documentation remained available for deeper reference after the course.

The project also improved alignment between academy content and the help center. Approved terminology and source content could be reused across both systems, making future learning materials easier to develop and maintain.
